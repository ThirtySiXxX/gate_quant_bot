## Output and limitations

The default-OFF callable is `research.qr015.dataset.assemble_local`; the module
CLI and complete request schema are documented in `research/qr015/README.md`.
It emits a versioned, hashed samples table; exclusion/asset/year/split/label
statistics; an information-interval inventory; and a COMPLETE manifest written
last. Failed outputs are retained without that manifest. `index.sqlite` is a
derived consumer index, not an economic account or authoritative portable file.

Row counts represent unique exported realized episode/proposal pairs. There is
one concrete upstream gap: `export_all` iterates realized episode groups and
nests their entry proposals; it provides no separately versioned unfilled/
rejected-proposal inventory. QR015 reports that count as null and explicitly
labels the proposal inventory episode-backed. It does not invent a QD schema,
silently call the missing count zero, or claim complete selection coverage.
Any future standalone-proposal handoff needs exact fields/schema and separate
allocation; the current consumer remains independently usable for the delivered
episode format.

Dependency diagnostics count interval connectivity across both coins, including
open-ended intervals. They are not an effective independent sample size or a
statistical sufficiency result. Training-only sign/distinct-target counts are
descriptive; no mean baseline, scaler, Ridge, calibration, CI or threshold search
runs. `training_ready=false` and `training_authorized=false` are unconditional.
Cache whole-file/source/ledger verification, finality correctness, and actual
data adequacy remain with their accepted upstream evidence and future scope.

Output limits are checked cooperatively; time/CPU/RSS require the calling run's
observations. Peak memory/throughput on an actual large export is unmeasured.
Per-row source windows are bounded, while the on-disk index grows with exported
episodes/intervals and statistics with their strata. All row/code/source identities
must be unchanged; unknown versions or corrupt data stop without fallback.
