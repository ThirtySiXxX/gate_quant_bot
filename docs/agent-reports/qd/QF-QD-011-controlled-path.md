   recheck before per-asset previous-hour capacity, reductions first, adverse

   actual-close plan/feature, waiting episodes and exactly-once completion keys.

   joint `allocate_portfolio`, native execution/timing and quantity conversion.
   It preserves approved x10 config, BTC timing-disabled/ETH timing-enabled,
   no duplicate x10, whole basket admission, unknown gating and close-first
   reversal/finality reentry. No fixed action or simplified strategy is substituted.
5. **reserve:** publishes one current held+pending reservation per instrument at
   the end of the same-time transaction, avoiding conflicting same-time reserve
   origins. Intermediate risk decisions derive current reserves from actual
   live lots/active orders and qualified MARK, not a stale reserve record.
